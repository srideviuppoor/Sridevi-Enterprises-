About Sri Devi Enterprises

A local business built on reliable service.

Sri Devi Enterprises has been serving Uppoor and the wider Udupi area since 1995, providing rental equipment and practical infrastructure for functions and gatherings.

The focus is simple: provide the equipment customers need, coordinate the requirements clearly and support the event with dependable local service.

From a few tables and chairs to larger requirements involving tents, stages, roofing, cooling, water facilities and transportation, customers can contact us directly to discuss their function.
